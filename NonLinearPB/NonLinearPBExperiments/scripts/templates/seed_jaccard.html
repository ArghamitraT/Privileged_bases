Reading note. At τ = 0 about half of all pixels brighten at every step, most of them black background moving by tiny amounts, and two random half-image sets already overlap with a Jaccard of about 0.35. Raising τ removes these tiny changes. A chance line is planned. AE caveat: AE coordinate order is arbitrary, so the same step in two AE seeds compares unrelated coordinates.

Changes are stored at half precision (float16, about 3 significant digits); positive changes too small for float16 are kept as the smallest positive value, so τ = 0 matches the saved results exactly.
